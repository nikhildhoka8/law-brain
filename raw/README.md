# raw/: your drop zone

Put your own class materials here, sorted into the folders below. Your agent reads
everything in here when you say "run the ingest prompt". These files stay on your
computer; the kit never uploads them anywhere.

| Folder | What goes in it |
|---|---|
| `syllabus/` | Your course syllabus and reading assignments |
| `cases/` | Your own case briefs, and short excerpts your professor handed out |
| `class-notes/` | Your notes from each class |
| `outlines/` | Outlines you wrote yourself |
| `exams/` | Practice exams and old exams your professor released |

Files can be PDF, Word (.docx), PowerPoint (.pptx), RTF, Markdown (.md), plain text
(.txt), saved emails (.eml), or scans and photos of handouts. If the agent can't read
something, it will tell you how to save it in a format it can.
